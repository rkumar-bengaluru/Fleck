# Demo library

Material Fleck answers from during a presales meeting. One folder per client, plus `_firm/` for firm-wide material that belongs to no single client.

| Folder | What it is | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `_firm/` | Rate card, capabilities, delivery FAQ for **Northbeam Digital**, a fictional consulting firm | Simulated |
| `larkspur-retail/` | A past engagement with a fictional retailer: proposal, SOW, case study | Simulated |
| `_reference/` | Real commercial contracts (MSA, hosting, maintenance, consulting, outsourcing) filed with the SEC, one folder per filing company. Reference only: Fleck may quote their terms as examples, never present them as Northbeam's past work | CUAD v1, see below |
| `jf/` | rupak.k's own SOWs | Private, not committed (see `.gitignore`) |

Simulated documents are invented for the demo. Names, numbers and outcomes are not real.

## Attribution

Contracts in `_reference/` are from the Contract Understanding Atticus Dataset (CUAD) v1 by The Atticus Project, licensed CC BY 4.0. https://www.atticusprojectai.org/cuad · https://zenodo.org/records/4595826. Files are renamed and unmodified. In those contracts the named companies are the parties, not Northbeam's clients.
