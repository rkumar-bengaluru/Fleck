# Delivery FAQ

Northbeam Digital.

Simulated document for the Fleck demo. Standard answers to questions clients ask in presales.

**How fast can you start?**
A pod of up to 6 people within 2 weeks of signing; up to 20 people within 4 weeks. Named architects can join discovery in the first week.

**What happens if a team member leaves?**
Replacement within 10 working days with a two-week overlap at our cost. Key people (architect, tech lead) are locked in for the first 6 months.

**Who owns the IP?**
The client owns all deliverables and code written for them on payment. Northbeam keeps its pre-existing accelerators and grants a perpetual licence to use them inside the delivered system.

**How do you handle data security?**
ISO 27001 controls; client data stays in the client's environment; no production data offshore without written approval; laptops are managed with disk encryption and DLP. Background checks for every engineer.

**What SLAs do you offer for support?**
P1: response 30 min, restore 4 h, 24×7. P2: response 2 h, restore 1 business day. P3: response 1 business day. Service credits up to 10% of the monthly support fee.

**Do you do fixed price?**
Yes, once scope is clear, usually after a 3–4 week paid discovery. Discovery is a fixed USD 25,000–45,000 depending on size.

**What is your warranty?**
90 days after go-live: defects against the agreed acceptance criteria are fixed at no cost.

**Which tools do you use?**
Jira and Confluence by default, or the client's own. GitHub or Azure DevOps for code and CI.

**How do you use GenAI in delivery?**
AI coding assistants are used under the client's approval and policy. No client code or data goes to a tool the client hasn't approved.

**Can you work with our in-house team?**
Yes. Mixed teams are common. We name a Northbeam tech lead per pod and follow the client's engineering standards.
