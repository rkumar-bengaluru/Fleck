A month is 160 billable hours. Hourly rate = monthly rate ÷ 160.

## Discounts

- Team of 10 or more for 6 months or more: 5%.
- Commitment of 12 months or more: a further 3%.
- Discounts stack to at most 8%. Anything beyond needs approval from the CFO.

## Fixed price

- Priced from the T&M estimate plus a 15–20% risk margin, depending on how clear the scope is.
- Milestone billing: 20% on signing, the rest split across milestones on acceptance.

## Other terms

- Payment: net 45 days.
- Travel for onsite work billed at cost, capped at 8% of fees unless agreed otherwise.
- Rates rise by at most 5% a year on multi-year deals.
- Minimum engagement: 3 months or USD 60,000.
