# Case study: Calloway & Marsh

Simulated document for the Fleck demo. Calloway & Marsh is a fictional law firm. Status: not approved to be named in writing. Describe it as "a mid-size law firm" until the client agrees.

## Challenge

Associates spent about 3 hours on the first-pass review of each NDA or MSA, checking every clause against the firm's playbook by hand. A generic AI review tool tried in 2025 was dropped within a month because its suggestions did not say where they came from.

## What Northbeam did

- A redline assistant inside the firm's existing document system. It reads an incoming agreement and proposes tracked changes against the firm's own playbook; every suggestion shows the playbook clause it came from.
- 5-week pilot on NDAs, then MSAs. Team of 5, 4 from Bengaluru.
- Associates accept or reject each change. Nothing is sent to the counterparty automatically.

## Results

- **First-pass review:** from about 3 hours to 40 minutes per agreement.
- **Volume:** 1,100 agreements reviewed in the first 6 months.
- **Accepted suggestions:** 78% of proposed changes accepted without edits.
- **Playbook sources:** 100% of suggestions carry a playbook reference.

## Client quote

"The partners trust it because it shows its working." Head of Contracts, Calloway & Marsh.

## What we'd do differently

We loaded the playbook as one long document and spent three weeks fixing clause matching. We would now split the playbook into one entry per clause position before the pilot starts.
