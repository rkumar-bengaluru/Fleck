# Statement of Work No. 2 under the Master Services Agreement dated 3 March 2025

Simulated document for the Fleck demo. Between Larkspur Retail Group, Inc. ("Client") and Northbeam Digital Pvt. Ltd. ("Supplier").

## 1. Scope

Supplier will design, build, test and cut over a cloud order management system ("New OMS") replacing Client's on-premise OMS, covering: order capture, sourcing and allocation, ship-from-store, BOPIS, returns, and integrations with SAP S/4HANA, Manhattan WMS and Salesforce Commerce Cloud.

Out of scope: changes inside SAP, Manhattan WMS or Salesforce; store hardware; data older than 3 years.

## 2. Term

1 April 2025 to 15 October 2025, then 8 weeks of hypercare.

## 3. Deliverables and acceptance

| # | Deliverable | Due | Acceptance |
|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | Solution architecture and integration contracts | 30 Apr 2025 | Signed off by Client architect |
| D2 | Order capture and sourcing in production for online orders | 30 Jun 2025 | 2 weeks without P1 defects |
| D3 | Ship-from-store and BOPIS, pilot of 20 stores | 15 Aug 2025 | Pilot KPIs met |
| D4 | Load test at 5,000 orders/minute | 1 Sep 2025 | p95 order placement under 400 ms |
| D5 | Rollout to all stores, legacy OMS read-only | 15 Oct 2025 | Client go-live sign-off |

Client has 5 business days to accept or reject each deliverable with reasons; silence counts as acceptance.

## 4. Fees and payment

Fixed price USD 1,420,000: 20% on signing, then 20% on each of D2–D5. Net 45 days. Change requests priced at the rate card in force.

## 5. Service levels during hypercare

P1 response 30 minutes, 24×7. Service credit of 2% of the hypercare fee per missed P1 response, capped at 10%.

## 6. Key personnel

Solution architect and both tech leads named in Annex A; not replaced without Client's consent except for resignation or illness.

## 7. Liability

Supplier's total liability under this SOW is capped at the fees paid under it in the preceding 12 months, excluding breach of confidentiality and data protection.
